## 1. Competitive Frame

- **Buyer's current alternative:** Tradify (per-user template quoting) — the most common tool in the ICP's hands for residential trade quoting in Western Sydney
- **Why that alternative falls short:** Tradify is a template editor that still requires the tradie to look up Bunnings prices, type every line item, and wait until they're home at a laptop — with the quote landing 24–72 hours after the site visit
- **Our category claim:** The fastest quote-to-win tool for Western Sydney trades — not a job management platform, not a US-built AI tool, not a spreadsheet upgrade

> The buyer is not choosing between SaaS products. They are choosing between "type it up tonight after dinner" and "done before I drive to the next job." Every word we write must make that choice visible.

## 6. Three Feature Blocks

Each block maps directly to a wedge workflow step.

---

### Block 1 — Photo → AI quote in 60 seconds
*(Wedge steps 2–4: snap photos, add one line, AI generates draft)*

**Today:** With Tradify or a Word template, you look up Bunnings prices, type every material line item, and guess your labour — at home, after dinner, 2–4 hours per quote.

**With QuotingAgent:** Snap 4–10 site photos, type one sentence ("re-roof, colorbond, single-storey"), and receive a fully itemised, Bunnings-priced, labour-rated draft in under 60 seconds — while you're still in the ute.

The quote includes current Bunnings live prices for materials, your own labour rates, the 10% GST line, and your NSW contractor licence number — automatically, on every quote, without you touching a keyboard.

---

### Block 2 — NSW-compliant by default, every time
*(Wedge step 4 compliance layer: licence binding + statutory warranty)*

**Today:** Manually copying your licence number into each quote (or forgetting, and risking an NSW Fair Trading warning — fines up to A$110,000 for unlicensed work quotes over A$5K).

**With QuotingAgent:** Your NSW contractor licence number and the mandatory NSW Home Building Act statutory warranty wording are stamped on every quote before it leaves your phone — verified against Service NSW at onboarding, never forgotten again.

This is the compliance floor that Tradify, ServiceM8, and every US AI quoting tool skip. We make it invisible and automatic so you can focus on winning the job, not managing paperwork.

---

### Block 3 — SMS-sent, one-tap accepted
*(Wedge steps 6–7: SMS delivery, read receipt, customer acceptance)*

**Today:** Email the PDF, wait 48–72 hours, follow up manually, lose the job to the painter who quoted on Tuesday while you were waiting for a reply.

**With QuotingAgent:** The approved quote goes out as an SMS with a tap-link — your customer reads it on their phone within minutes and can accept with one tap. You get a push notification when they open it and another when they accept. The winning contractor is whichever one quotes first; now that's you.

Auto-follow-up SMS nudges fire at D+3 and D+7 for any lead that hasn't responded — so no job goes cold while you're busy on the tools.

## 8. Voice & Tone Lock

### Axis positions

| Axis | Position | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Confident ↔ Humble | **Confident** — makes the claim and owns it | The 5-minute promise is either true or it isn't; hedging sounds like we don't believe our own product |
| Technical ↔ Plain | **Plain, leaning technical only for proof** — "Bunnings live pricing" is technical and trusted; "AI-powered platform" is not | ICP is smartphone-native but allergic to SaaS jargon |
| Warm ↔ Formal | **Warm** — peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-customer | A tradie from Penrith is not a "valued customer"; they're Rob |
| Witty ↔ Sober | **Witty-leaning** — dry Australian humour, not puns | "Kitchen table at 9pm" is a joke that lands because it's true |
| Specific ↔ Abstract | **Specific, always** — name the suburb, name the tool, name the time | "Under 5 minutes" beats "fast"; "Bunnings" beats "leading suppliers" |

### Vocabulary

**Use:**
- "quote" (not "estimate," not "proposal")
- "ute" / "on the tools" / "on site" (authentic Australian trade vernacular)
- "crew" (not "team," not "staff," not "users")
- "job" (not "project," not "engagement")
- "win the job" (outcome-first, matches how tradies think)
- "A$99/month" (always AUD prefix, no ambiguity)

**Avoid:**
- "AI-powered," "leverage," "platform," "solution," "seamless," "streamline," "unlock," "empower," "next-generation," "world-class," "end-to-end"
- "clients" (they're customers or homeowners)
- any passive voice on the value prop ("quotes are generated" → "get a quote")
- US spellings ("labor" → "labour"; "color" → "colour")

### Reference brand voice

**Target:** Linear (confident, specific, fast — respects the user's intelligence) crossed with **Atlassian's older voice** (plain Australian, no US-marketing-speak) and filtered through **trades vernacular** (direct, no-nonsense, outcome-driven).

Not: Notion (too cosy), Vercel (too developer-abstract), Mailchimp (too playful for a compliance-sensitive workflow).

Review of all sections: no scheduling claims, no invoicing claims, no enterprise claims, no project management claims. The anti-positioning section (Section 7) explicitly names what we do not do. **Pass.**
